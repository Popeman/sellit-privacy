Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

Sell It is committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

The App stores all data locally on your device using IndexedDB. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers.

Photos

Photos are processed on-device. If you enable Smart Photos, images are sent to our API for listing suggestions and are not stored after processing.

eBay Integration

We use OAuth 2.0 authentication. We do not store your eBay password. Access tokens are stored locally.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device.

Analytics

The App does not include third-party analytics or tracking.

Data Deletion

Delete the App to permanently remove all data.
